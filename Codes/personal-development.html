The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

The phrase "Lean Purse" can be defined as having little amounts of money, this chapter talks about several habits I can implement in my life to build future wealth. It speaks about saving at least a tenth of your earnings, differentiating my needs from my wants, investing my money or putting it to work so it can multiply, ensuring to not lose it, reflecting on what I have right now that can be useful, securing a future income as I prepare for old age as well as financially securing my family and lastly practice my ability to earn money through my occupation or side hustles. For me, some habits I can take from this is differentiating my needs like clothes and food from my wants such as purchasing games and subscriptions as well as securing my earnings by placing it on my card that is not able to be used online. Additionally, I can use my laptop to have a side hustle to secure a future income apart from my career in computing. Gaining a side hustle is very important as it can assist me in life when I’m either unemployed or when money is short.

The Five Laws of Gold

This chapter is similar to the previous one as it also teaches you how to build future wealth by teaching how money behaves. It tells me that it grows for the one who saves so set a side a portion of my earnings, works for those who are wise so I mustn’t be foolish but wise with my money, seek advice from persons with wisdom about money, be cautious in what I choose to invest in and distance myself from those who chase earnings that seem impossible. Personally, I should start seeking more advice from people who are wise with their money to benefit me in the future as well as knowing who earns money by rigging systems or deceptive ways to stay away from them. I live in a country where most music is about making money through deceptive and law-breaking methods so I need to be careful who I choose to connect with.

My Biggest Takeaway

The main thing I would say I learn from these two chapters is to ensure to build future wealth as early as I can and secure it the best I can. Using lessons from the two, utilizing my laptop to gain a side hustle to assist with my future income and ensuring that I distance myself from persons who use illegal or addictive ways such as gambling or scamming to earn money are the most significant lessons for me.
