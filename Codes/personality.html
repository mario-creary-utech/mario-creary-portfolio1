Personality

My Trait and Hobbies

  1. Reading my Bible

    This matters to me because it is one of the most important habits for building my relationship with Christ, and it makes me a better person by giving me wisdom. It lets me reflect on my life and teaches me to deny myself and become a loving individual. It also helps me in school: it helps me not to stress about schoolwork or exams, nd it teaches me to be honest about my work, with no cheating, no excessive use of AI for assignments, and more.

  2. Listening to Music

    This is important to me because I have loved music since I was young, and now that I have transitioned to listening to Christian music, it keeps me motivated to push forward and renew my mind to be more Christ-like. Since I started at UTECH, I have listened to more of this genre, and it has helped me focus on my assignments, get through stressful times, and keep pursuing the degree that God has led me to.

  3. Being Kind

    This trait has benefited many people, including me, because when I am kind to others, I find myself not hurting their feelings. Also, since Christ said I should be more like Him, being kind helps people see Him through me. It benefits me at school too, because students and even visitors sometimes need help, and it doesn't hurt to be kind. I do not need to be repaid for my kindness, because I believe it should be free.

My Saviour

Jesus Christ

Jesus is a real person who was born to a virgin in Bethlehem and grew up in Nazareth. He walked the earth, preached, and performed miracles. According to the Gospels, He lived a sinless life and died on the cross for sinners, and after three days He rose from the dead and appeared to many people, including more than 500 at one time (1 Corinthians 15:6). His life was so important in history that the New Testament has thousands of surviving manuscript copies, more than 5,000 in Greek alone. In His time, He preached the Good News, changed people's lives, healed the sick, and began a new covenant so that we may be saved through Him.

Personally, He is my hero because He prevented me from seeing my life as worthless and changed my life for the better. Even though I did not study enough for CSEC, He ensured I passed all my subjects with Grades 1 and 2 and created pathways for me that I could not even imagine. Right now, He is molding me into a better person by teaching me to be kind, loving, joyful, peaceful, gentle, faithful, good and self-controlled. He is my Creator, Father, Lover and best friend.

"But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you." Jesus, Matthew 6:33 (ESV)
